About Us

Our Passion for Baking

At Wheels Bakery, we believe the best days start with warm bread and freshly baked treats. The name "Wheels" represents our rolling pins in motion and our early-morning delivery carts reaching neighborhoods across town.

We source 100% organic flour and local dairy to craft artisanal bread, flaky croissants, and custom cakes daily. Whether you stop by our shop or order online for delivery, we aim to bring comfort with every bite.
